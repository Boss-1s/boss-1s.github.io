About a year ago, I made a song. The title of this song, which I'm fine with sharing, is **_Remember Love_** — or, in Chinese, **_《記住愛》_**. I have received many positive feedbacks about this song, especially with my close friends and my family, so, after a year of thought, I have decided that I will release this song **as my debut single.** Not bad for a guy who spends more time working on his niche code than doing homework, eh?

## About the song

I won't share much about the song itself. That would be cheating, eh? What I can say, however, is just random stuff about it:
- It's in the key of F
- It's in **8/4 time**
- The main tempo is set at 168 BPM.

For all you music nerds out there, you can probably imagine it. If you're not a music nerd....well, I guess you're just gonna have to be patient.

## Why one year?

To be completely honest, I could've gotten the song recorded and produced a long, long time ago. Probably. But, I did NOT have the necessary resources to record nor produce, so that song's been stuck within my hands and in my voice, but separately. However, now that I **do** have those resources, it's time to put the two together.

Not necessarily at the same time though - I suck at playing and singing at the same time for some reason.

## What's next?

I'm co-hosting a collab that I will not leak. It involves like 10 other amateurs that I know who are just like me: we have no idea what we're doing, we're just doing it. Music-wise, that's probably the next major thing you'll here from me. After that, comes my debut album, which _Remember Love_ will be re-released and included in.

Of course, I will also still be working on `kms` and `scratchattach` and of course, my schoolwork.
