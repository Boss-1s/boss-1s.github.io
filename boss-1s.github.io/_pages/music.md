### Back On Track

Ah yes, my first public ~~post~~ piece on Noteflight was this decent arrangement of `Back On Track`. I have been and still am really into Geometry Dash, so this was a great piece to start with.

> [!note]
> One thing to note was that this piece has bad bass lines. For some reason my younger self decided to auto-correct the chords so it went
>
  > `F` -> `Eb` -> `Bb` -> `F`
  >
  > instead of matching the original progression,
  >
  > `F` -> `Eb` -> `F` -> `Bb`
>
> . As you can see the last two are swapped. Not really important if you're just a random listener, it's just a little tibit that seemed sharable. As I said earlier, "remnants of a younger me".

Also note this arrangement is based off of the "Back On Track" **as heard *in Geometry Dash***.

> [!tip] Description
> This is a band arrangement for the song from the Geometry Dash level "Back On Track". The piano part (melody) is interchangeable with the other instruments and can be played as solos.<br>
><br>
> Parts:<br>
> Piano<br>
> Flute 1&2<br>
> Trumpet<br>
> Percussion<br>

`One Snowy Beginning` is a duet I made back in September. Written in the key of F and just over a minute long, the piece captures a beginning -- a cold and wintry, yet warm, beginning of a story. *This piece teases one of the songs coming out in my debut album (whose name I will not reveal yet), with the following line in the description:* **`Time to time again, \ what once was will be again.`** Hee hee hee....
